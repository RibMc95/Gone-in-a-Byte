Connect Your Square Account

To process payments through Gone in a Bitee, you need to authorize our application to access your Square merchant account.

  1. Click the button below.
  2. Log in to your Square account.
  3. Review the requested permissions.
  4. Click Allow to connect.
Connect with Square

You will be redirected to Square's secure authorization page. Gone in a Bitee never sees your Square password.
